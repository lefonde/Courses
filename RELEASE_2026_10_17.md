## Change and learner benefit

- The lowercase **erdős** name, selected v13 light portrait and dedicated dark v3 portrait replace the compass branding. A brief wink plays on hover or keyboard focus. Reduced-motion preferences and touch-only input retain the static portrait.
- A compact mobile header leaves more space for learning. Its icon-only menu opens labeled navigation, appearance settings and backup controls. Desktop navigation is more compact and uses consistent SVG icons.
- Permanent browser-storage remarks and redundant sidebar captions are removed. Save failures remain explicit and persistent until a successful save or import.
- Existing four appearances, lesson content, routes, identifiers, storage schema, source files and student progress are preserved. No private course PDFs or progress exports are included.
